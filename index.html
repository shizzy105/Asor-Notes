01REF — insert scripture references

Type the reference in whatever shorthand you already use.

Tap "+ Reference" and type it however you'd naturally write it — "Matt 3.5", "Matt 3 v6", "Matthew 3:5". The insert tool reads the shorthand, confirms the match, and drops in a properly formatted chip right where your cursor is.

  • Forgiving syntax — dots, colons, and "v" are all read the same way.
  • Instant validation — a confirmation shows the resolved reference before you insert it.
  • One-tap peek — open a distraction-free passage card without losing your place.
  • Four translations on device — KJV, NKJV, ESV, and WEB, switchable mid-read.
  • Insert whole passages — copy a single verse or a full range straight into your note.
02LEX — Strong's concordance

Every root word, in the original Hebrew and Greek.

8,674 Hebrew entries and 5,624 Greek entries live on your device — no connection required, in the middle of a sermon or on a plane.

  • Same insert tool — type a Strong's number like H799 or G26 and it resolves to a chip the same way a scripture reference does.
  • Original script & transliteration — the lemma, phonetic pronunciation, and etymology, together.
  • Exhaustive definition & KJV usage — occurrence counts included, so you can see the word's full footprint.
  • Insert straight to note — send any entry into your current entry without retyping it.
03JRN — multimedia journaling

Notes that hold your whole process — not just your typing.

Voice memos for sermons and spoken prayers. Freehand sketches for mind maps and Hebrew character studies. Photos of handwritten outlines and church bulletins. All of it embeds inline, alongside your text and reference chips, in one block-based note.

  • Built-in audio recorder — with a scrubbable waveform and speed control up to 2×.
  • Freehand canvas — adjustable brush sizes, color palette, undo and redo.
  • Photo attachments — sermon outlines and study materials, embedded where you need them.
  • Full formatting — bold, italic, underline, alignment, and multi-level headings.

05PRV — privacy & offline

No account. No sync server. No one reading over your shoulder.

Everything — your notes, your Bible, your concordance, your recordings — lives on your device from the first launch. There's nothing to sign up for and nothing being tracked.

  • No login required — open the app and start writing immediately.
  • Complete Bible bundled — all 66 books, plus the full Hebrew & Greek dictionaries, work with no signal at all.
  • One-click backup — export every note, photo, recording, and sketch into a single portable .zip file.
  • 30-day recovery — deleted notes wait in a Recently Deleted screen before they're gone for good.
